# RC3.9.30 Prime Cost Daily Labor Allocation

- Official payroll remains Monday-Sunday and is not rewritten.
- Prime Cost uses exact selected dates.
- Fixed weekly Kitchen, Dishwasher, Busser/BOH and Management wages are allocated evenly across 7 calendar days.
- Partial weeks include only overlapping days.
- Daily/manual payroll remains assigned to its actual date.
- Customer tips remain excluded from Prime Cost.
- Prime Cost drawer identifies daily labor allocations.
