# RC6.18 Professional Kitchen Group Payroll Popup

- Kitchen Group Payroll opens a professional card-style popup.
- Green Kitchen Group Payroll and purple Manual Payroll buttons remain distinct.
- Automatically defaults to the selected Monday-Sunday payroll week.
- Shows selected employee count and weekly payroll summary.
- Supports adding any active employee to the current kitchen payroll batch.
- Supports selecting/removing employees before creating payroll.
- Includes Reset to Group Defaults and employee search.
- Creates only the employees selected in the popup.
