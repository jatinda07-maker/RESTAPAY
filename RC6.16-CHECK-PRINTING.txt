RC6.16 - Payroll Check Printing

- Select approved payroll rows and click Print Checks.
- Only non-void rows with payment type Check are printed.
- Optional sequential check numbering fills blank check numbers.
- Prints a letter-size check plus payroll stub and employee copy.
- Saves business/check-print settings without storing bank account or routing numbers.
- Can automatically mark printed payroll Paid and store the check date/number.
