# RC6.19 Approved Payroll Batch Payment Popup

- Replaces the basic Edit Selected form with a professional card-style payment workspace.
- Displays selected employee count, batch total, and pay date.
- Provides card options for Pending, Paid, Void, and Keep Current.
- Provides card options for Check, Cash, ACH, Card, Other, and Keep Current.
- Supports check number, paid date, and a batch payment note.
- Shows every selected employee and amount before changes are applied.
