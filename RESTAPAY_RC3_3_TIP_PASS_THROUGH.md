# RESTAPAY RC3.3 - Tip Pass-Through Accounting Fix

- Employee-owned tips remain visible in payroll/payment history.
- Tracks Tips Earned, Tips Withheld (3.5%), and Net Tips Paid separately.
- Net tip checks are excluded from operating labor used by Prime Cost and Operating Profit.
- Regular wages, overtime, and extra pay remain operating labor.
- Financial reconciliation and labor percentage now use operating labor, while Payroll Total remains the actual payment total for audit/reconciliation.
