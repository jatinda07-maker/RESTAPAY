# RC6.20 Centered Group Payroll Modal

- Kitchen Group Payroll now opens as a true fixed modal centered in the visible browser window.
- No page scrolling is required to find the popup.
- Closing the X, Cancel, or clicking the dark backdrop closes the modal.
- The employee table has its own vertical and horizontal scrolling area.
- Header, payroll period controls, add-employee controls, and footer remain visible.
- All employee rows and right-side fields can be reached without the modal being trimmed.
