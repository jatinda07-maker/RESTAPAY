RESTAPAY RC6.15 - Monday-Sunday Weekly Payroll Fix

Fixes:
- Payroll import now groups by Employee + Sunday week-ending date.
- Each Monday through Sunday week creates one separate row per employee.
- Multi-week Toast reports no longer collapse the whole report into one employee row.
- Pay date and period end are always Sunday.
- Period start is always Monday.
- Multiple shifts or daily rows within the same week are merged once.

Example:
2026-07-20 through 2026-07-26 -> pay date 2026-07-26
2026-07-27 through 2026-08-02 -> pay date 2026-08-02
