# RESTAPAY RC3.7 Financial Reconciliation

- Prime Cost and Labor Mix exclude employee-owned tip pass-through.
- Employer labor is split into BOH operating labor, management payroll, FOH wages, and unmapped/review payroll.
- Assistant/general managers are handled through department allocation rather than BOH operating-labor entries.
- Prime Cost Entries no longer route to tip entries; they show direct Food/Alcohol purchases plus employer-funded payroll entries.
- Food Cost and Alcohol Cost drawers use the same full allocated department economics as Food vs Alcohol Cost.
- Business Expenses By Category groups the selected-period rows by actual Expense Type/Category, A-Z.
- Payroll Total By Category groups payroll by job/duty.
- Tip Pass-Through is visually marked with a light-red excluded treatment.
- KPI and summary cards use slightly larger typography and a subtle darker/tinted background while keeping the left navigation unchanged.
