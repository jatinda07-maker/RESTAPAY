# RESTAPAY RC3.7.2 - KPI, Date Range, Reports, and Cash Reconciliation

- KPI drawers now use real underlying datasets for vendor counts, invoice category totals, Price Increase cards, and report cards.
- Detail drawer explicit-entry lists are filtered by the active drawer date range.
- Report preview cards route to reconciled Sales, Payroll, Expense, and P&L detail views.
- Prime Cost includes BOH/kitchen labor plus management payroll once; employee tip pass-through remains excluded.
- Remaining Cash carries forward all prior recorded cash activity into the selected period.
- Cash Withdrawal can be recorded with date, amount, purpose, and notes. Withdrawals persist through the Expenses collection, are excluded from operating expense/P&L totals, and reduce Remaining Cash.
- Cash Flow and Reports show Carry Forward and Cash Withdrawals explicitly.
