# RC6.25 Simplified Final Payroll

- Removed Approved Payroll from navigation and application routing.
- Creating Toast, manual, or group payroll now produces final paid history records immediately.
- Toast hours remain visible for reference but do not create hourly wages.
- Tipped payroll formula: Final Tips + Extra Pay.
- Fixed/salary payroll formula: Fixed Pay + Extra Pay.
- Removed hidden hourly-rate fallback from final payroll calculations.
- Payroll History supports direct editing of employee, pay date, fixed pay, tips, extra pay, payment method, check number, and deletion.
- Fixed the employee-specific Final Payroll mismatch seen for Lucas H and similar Toast employees.
